**If your research involves human participants, you must obtain approval from the AIT Ethics Committee before you start collecting data.**

This includes interviews, surveys, user studies, and experiments with people. Discuss your study with your supervisor early so you have time to prepare the ethics application and receive approval before data collection begins.

## How to apply

1. **Get the ethics form.** Download the Word example below and prepare your application with your supervisor.
2. **Send your completed application** to [research.ethics@ait.asia](mailto:research.ethics@ait.asia).

**Wait for approval before starting data collection.**

## Example application

[Download the example ethics application (Word)](/downloads/ait-ethics-application-example.docx). It includes a completed study protocol, a sample participant consent form, and draft reviewer questions. Use it as a reference when preparing your own application.

## Research reimbursement

- **Participant payments.** Ask each paid participant to complete and sign the [AIT participant receipt form (Word)](/downloads/ait-participant-receipt.docx) and return it to you. Include the completed receipt with your reimbursement request.
- **Before you buy.** Ask [FAST Finance](mailto:fast_finance@ait.ac.th) whether the expense is eligible for reimbursement.
- **API and equipment purchases.** Use your AIT email where applicable and ask the vendor to issue the receipt in the name of **Asian Institute of Technology**. Return any equipment bought with AIT funds to AIT.
- **Personal purchases.** Personal items and subscriptions such as a mouse, laptop, or ChatGPT subscription are generally ineligible. If you need one for your research, speak with your supervisor before buying it.
- **Submitting a claim.** Send your receipts to [fast_finance@ait.ac.th](mailto:fast_finance@ait.ac.th) or contact FAST Finance for help with the reimbursement process.
