**If your research involves human participants, you must obtain approval from the AIT Ethics Committee before you start collecting data.**

This includes interviews, surveys, user studies, and experiments with people. Discuss your study with your supervisor early so you have time to prepare the ethics application and receive approval before data collection begins.

## How to apply

1. **Get the ethics form.** Download the Word example below and prepare your application with your supervisor.
2. **Send your completed application** to [research.ethics@ait.asia](mailto:research.ethics@ait.asia).

### Start date and data collection

Section 17 (Duration of Project) of the ethics application states:

> The start date should not be within the 2 months after the submission of this application, to allow for clearance to be processed.

When you submit your application, confirm that data collection has not yet begun and will begin only after you obtain clearance from the AIT Research Ethics Review Committee (RERC).

## Example application

[Download the example ethics application (Word)](/downloads/ait-ethics-application-example.docx). It includes a completed study protocol, a sample participant consent form, and draft reviewer questions. Use it as a reference when preparing your own application.
