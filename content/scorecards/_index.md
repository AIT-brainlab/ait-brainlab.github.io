## Novelty Check

- What is new compared with the closest verified work?
- Why does that difference matter? A new tool, combination, or domain alone is not enough.
- What would another researcher learn from this work?
- Have you checked the strongest counterexample to your novelty claim?

## Validity Check

- Does the evidence measure what you claim? Define the concepts and their measures.
- Are the baseline, information, effort, and resources fair?
- Are hypotheses, IVs, DVs, controls, and analysis clear where applicable?
- Could confounds, leakage, sampling, or another explanation account for the result?
- Are uncertainty, failures, limitations, and enough detail to reproduce the work reported?

## Writing Check

- Can you state the question and contribution clearly in one sentence?
- Do the question, method, results, and conclusion form one argument?
- Does each important claim have evidence, with verified references and numbers?
- Do figures and terminology match the text?
- Are planned work, actual findings, and interpretations clearly distinguished?
- Are novelty and limitations explained honestly, without overselling?
