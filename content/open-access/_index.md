## Elsevier

Eligible authors can publish open access in **295 participating Elsevier journals at 45% of the list price** for the article processing charge (APC).

The 55% discount applies when all co-authors are affiliated with institutions in countries in the same eligible discount group. Check your journal and co-author affiliations before relying on the discount.

[Check the eligible journal titles and conditions on the AIT Library website](https://library.ait.ac.th/2026/01/20/publish-open-access-with-elsevier-at-45-of-the-list-price-title-list/).
