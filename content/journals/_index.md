Choose a publication venue by the contribution and evidence your work offers. This page includes journals and relevant conferences. Scope comes first; check current author instructions, deadlines, publication charges, and institutional agreements before submission.

## Choose the venue by the contribution

The same topic can produce different contributions. Choose the readers who need your finding.

| Negotiation research contributes… | Look for a venue that values… |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence about user decisions and control | Human–AI interaction, behaviour, or interface design. |
| A new language-model method or evaluation | NLP methods, resources, or language-model behaviour. |
| A general learning method with broad evidence | Machine-learning methods, theory, or substantial empirical insight. |

**Before selecting a venue:** Find two recent papers with a contribution and evidence pattern similar to yours. Explain why their readership would need your result. Neither an AI topic nor a prestigious venue name establishes fit. A specialist journal may be the right home for a focused contribution.

## Leading conferences

| Conference | Good fit when… |
| --- | --- |
| [CHI](https://chi.acm.org/) · Human–computer interaction | The main contribution concerns people, interaction, design, or human–AI systems. |
| [ACL](https://www.aclweb.org/portal/) · Natural language processing | The work advances language models, NLP methods, resources, evaluation, or understanding. |
| [NeurIPS](https://neurips.cc/) · Machine learning and AI | The paper offers strong technical or scientific insight with rigorous evaluation. |
| [ICML](https://icml.cc/) · Machine learning | The central contribution is a machine-learning method, theory, analysis, or substantial empirical result. |

## HCI journals: Core and interactive systems

| Journal | Good fit when… |
| --- | --- |
| [ACM TOCHI](https://dl.acm.org/journal/tochi) | The paper makes a strong, general HCI contribution. |
| [ACM TiiS](https://dl.acm.org/journal/tiis) | Interaction with intelligent systems is central. |
| [IJHCS](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/international-journal-of-human-computer-studies) | The work connects interaction with cognition or human factors. |
| [IJHCI](https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/hihc20) | The paper offers broad or applied HCI evidence. |
| [Behaviour & Information Technology](https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/tbit20) | Technology use, work, behavior, or social context is central. |

## Applied HCI journals: Health, behavior, and education

| Journal | Good fit when… |
| --- | --- |
| [ACM Transactions on Computing for Healthcare](https://dl.acm.org/journal/health) | The work makes a computing contribution that improves healthcare, such as digital health, clinical AI, sensing, or health interaction. |
| [Computers in Human Behavior](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/computers-in-human-behavior) | The contribution explains behavior, well-being, or social effects of technology. |
| [Computers & Education](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/computers-and-education) | The paper makes a strong learning-science or educational-technology contribution. |
| [ACM TOCE](https://dl.acm.org/journal/toce) | The research advances computing education. |
| [Education and Information Technologies](https://link.springer.com/journal/10639) | The work studies educational technology in authentic contexts. |

## NLP and machine-learning journals

| Journal | Good fit when… |
| --- | --- |
| [Transactions of the ACL](https://direct.mit.edu/tacl) | The paper presents substantial, original NLP work with strong evidence. |
| [Computational Linguistics](https://direct.mit.edu/coli) | The work offers deep computational-linguistic insight or comprehensive analysis. |
| [Journal of Machine Learning Research](https://jmlr.org) | The contribution is a mature ML method, theory, resource, or extensive study. |
| [Transactions on Machine Learning Research](https://jmlr.org/tmlr/) | The original ML contribution is technically sound and benefits from open review. |
