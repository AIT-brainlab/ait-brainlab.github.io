Choose a publication venue by the contribution and evidence your work offers. This page includes journals and relevant conferences. Scope comes first; check current author instructions, deadlines, publication charges, and institutional agreements before submission.

## Choose the venue by the contribution

The same topic can produce different contributions. Choose the readers who need your finding.

| Negotiation research contributes… | Look for a venue that values… |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence about user decisions and control | Human–AI interaction, behaviour, or interface design. |
| A new language-model method or evaluation | NLP methods, resources, or language-model behaviour. |
| A general learning method with broad evidence | Machine-learning methods, theory, or substantial empirical insight. |

**Before selecting a venue:** Find two recent papers with a contribution and evidence pattern similar to yours. Explain why their readership would need your result. Neither an AI topic nor a prestigious venue name establishes fit. A specialist journal may be the right home for a focused contribution.

## Leading conferences

| Conference | Good fit when… |
| --- | --- |
| [CHI](https://chi.acm.org/) · Human–computer interaction | The main contribution concerns people, interaction, design, or human–AI systems. |
| [ACL](https://www.aclweb.org/portal/) · Natural language processing | The work advances language models, NLP methods, resources, evaluation, or understanding. |
| [NeurIPS](https://neurips.cc/) · Machine learning and AI | The paper offers strong technical or scientific insight with rigorous evaluation. |
| [ICML](https://icml.cc/) · Machine learning | The central contribution is a machine-learning method, theory, analysis, or substantial empirical result. |

**Checked 30 October 2026.** IF values show their metric year. Linked **h-index** values are OpenAlex lifetime counts retrieved on this date, not SCImago or Google Scholar scores. Incomplete coverage is marked unverified.

**Timing:** first decision includes desk decisions; post-review decision means the first external-review cycle. Neither guarantees acceptance. Revisions add time; unverified entries link to current guidance.

**Free to read is not always free to publish.** APC means article processing charge. For hybrid journals, the subscription route has no OA APC; optional open access may be paid or covered by an agreement. Check other possible charges and funder requirements before choosing the route.

**ACM in 2026:** all publications are open access. Eligible institutional coverage or waivers can remove the author payment; otherwise subsidised journal APCs are **US$1,450**, or **US$950 with at least one ACM/SIG member**. Do not assume AIT coverage without checking. These 2026 rates are temporary. [ACM author guidance](https://prod-authors.acm.bloomreach.cloud/open-access/acm-open-for-authors-home) · [pricing and 2027 changes](https://prod-libraries.acm.bloomreach.cloud/acmopen/apc-list-pricing).

Metric and fee sources: [IJHCI](https://www.tandfonline.com/action/journalInformation?journalCode=hihc20), [BIT](https://www.tandfonline.com/action/journalInformation?journalCode=tbit20), [EAIT](https://link.springer.com/journal/10639). For Elsevier's optional OA prices, obtain a current quote from the journal's author guidance; its insights pages could not be verified during this check.
